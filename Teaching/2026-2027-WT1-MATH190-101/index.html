Description

The course Calculus Survey will treat, among others, functions, limits, asymptotes, derivatives, related rates, Riemann sums, and integrals. The prerequisites required for this course are covered in Pre-calculus 12.

The course will have weekly classes on

  • Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays in IRC, room G3, from 14:00 to 14:50.

The book we will be following is Single Variable Calculus (2nd edition) by Jonathan D. Rogawski. We will be covering material in Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8. The books CLP-1 Differential Calculus and CLP-2 Integral Calculus by Joel Feldman, Andrew Rechnitzer, and Elyse Yeager, are useful references for the material covered in the course. We will be covering material in CLP-1 Sections 0.4, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 2.4, 2.6, 2.7, 2.8, 2.9, 2.10, 3.2, and 4.1, and material in CLP-2 Sections 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.5, 1.6, 1.7, and 1.8. You may find all the information you will need on the Syllabus and on Canvas, and you may ask any questions you have on Piazza.
